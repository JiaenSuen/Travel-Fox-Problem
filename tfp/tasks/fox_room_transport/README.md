# FOX-RM-L3 — Fox Room Transport · Doors (V5)

A long-horizon multi-room POMDP in which the fox must find one seeded cargo object, operate stateful doors, and deliver the cargo to a destination in another room. Packaged layouts span **15×15 to 31×31** and 4 to 16 rooms; room geometry, door positions, cargo/destination cells, and initial door states vary across map × seed episodes.

## Why V4 could remain at 0% success

V4 coupled a difficult long-horizon task with a weak navigation representation and door rewards that were not tied to route usefulness. In particular, opening any door earned a direct bonus while closing it was penalized less, so local open/close behavior could compete with actual transport. Structural distance also treated closed doors as traversable and therefore did not credit the required `TOGGLE_DOOR` action as true path progress.

V5 removes all three V4 models and rewards. The task now uses one learning reward and three task-specific models built around the same controlled objective.

## V5 navigation signal

The observation remains a **local 5×5 or 7×7 crop**. Channels 7/8 replace the V4 straight-line-through-walls target vector with a **shortest-route doorway waypoint**. Internally, the environment identifies only the first doorway on the current executable shortest path; the policy receives its normalized 2-D offset, or the cargo/destination offset when no doorway remains. It does **not** receive the global map, room graph, complete path, next movement action, or distance field. The CNN must still learn local obstacle avoidance and the policy must learn when to operate a door.

## V5 models

| Model | Design | Intended advantage |
|---|---|---|
| `001_route_prior_cnn` | Residual route-prior logits + separate CNN geometry encoder + explicit route/task-context MLP | Prevents low-bandwidth waypoint, carrying, and door-state cues from being diluted as image channels. Strongest simple baseline. |
| `002_route_prior_action_memory` | Residual route prior + structured fusion + learnable 10-action history | Adds compact behavior memory for doorway retries, backtracking, and short oscillations without recurrent image memory. |
| `003_route_prior_gru_memory` | Residual route prior + structured fusion + action memory + 96-D GRU | Maintains longer room/door history for perceptual aliasing and multi-room return paths. Uses truncated-BPTT PPO with exact rollout context replay. |

All three models use the same reward so model comparisons do not confound architecture and reward design.

## Reward — `001_actionable_geodesic`

V5 uses **shortest executable action cost** rather than structural cell distance. Moving through floor/open-door cells costs one action; crossing a closed door costs `TOGGLE + MOVE`, so a useful door opening reduces distance by one action and receives progress automatically. Opening an irrelevant door gives no special bonus, while closing a required door increases distance and cancels earlier progress. This removes the V4 door-toggle reward loop.

The reward is:

- `-0.010` per step;
- `+0.22 × (D_before − D_after)` using actionable geodesic distance;
- `-0.18` invalid-action penalty and `-1.0` early-drop penalty;
- `-0.025` repeated-state penalty after persistent revisits;
- `+3.0` pickup milestone and `+12.0` successful delivery.

Distance shaping is symmetric: moving one action toward the active target and later undoing it produces approximately cancelling progress terms rather than free reward.

## Development sanity check — not benchmark data

Before packaging, V5 was checked specifically to ensure the redesign had escaped the structural 0%-success regime. A temporary `001_route_prior_cnn` checkpoint trained for only **8,192 PPO steps on the Small training tier** reached **100%** on the unseen-Small quick evaluation. The same temporary checkpoint was then greedily checked over the complete packaged **20 test maps × 5 seeds** matrix and completed **99/100 episodes** (Small 20/20, Medium 20/20, Large 19/20, Large+ 20/20, Large++ 20/20; mean 61.28 steps). Separate 1,024-step pipeline runs verified that both the Action-Memory and GRU variants complete PPO optimization and solve their smoke suite.

These are **development diagnostics only**. Temporary checkpoints are not shipped, these measurements do not populate `results/FOX-RM-L3`, and the Controlled Benchmark table remains driven only by complete evaluation records generated by the repository protocol.

## Scale curriculum

| Tier | Map size | Room lattice | Train / test |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Small | 15×15 | 2×2 | 10 / 4 |
| Medium | 19×19 | 2×3 | 10 / 4 |
| Large | 23×23 | 3×3 | 10 / 4 |
| Large+ | 27×27 | 3×4 | 10 / 4 |
| Large++ | 31×31 | 4×4 | 10 / 4 |

Training presets progressively unlock map-size groups and use 7×7 perception with the `task` action mask. Formal evaluation remains the fixed **20 test maps × 5 seeds = 100 episodes** controlled protocol. Smoke runs and partial experiments must not enter the README benchmark table.
